FH2 YouTube Radio includes these open-source Rust libraries, compiled into D3DCompiler_47.dll.
MPL-2.0 libraries (symphonia audio decoders) are used unmodified; their source is at the repository listed.
yt-dlp and deno are NOT included: the mod downloads them from their official GitHub releases into
youtube-radio\bin on first start; they keep their own licenses (yt-dlp: Unlicense, deno: MIT).
The cast pairing follows the protocol as implemented by yt-cast-receiver (MIT, github.com/patrickkfkan/yt-cast-receiver).
